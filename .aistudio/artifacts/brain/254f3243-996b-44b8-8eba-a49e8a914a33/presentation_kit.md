> *"Hello everyone! Have you ever wondered why sportsbooks make tens of billions of dollars every year? Today, I'm introducing **Edge Scanner**—an analytics suite that shifts the math back in favor of everyday sports fans through transparent data, situational intelligence, and bankroll discipline."*

### 0:25 – 0:55 | Slide 1: Multi-Sportsbook Line Shopping
> *"Just like you’d never buy a flight ticket without checking Google Flights, you shouldn't place a bet without checking who offers the best price. In **Edge Scanner**, we pull authorized live lines from DraftKings and FanDuel side-by-side. If DraftKings has Kansas City at +1.5, but FanDuel offers +2.0 at +112 odds, Edge Scanner highlights the green edge indicator. ### 0:55 – 1:25 | Slide 2 & 3: Matchup Analytics & Stadium Weather
> *"Second, we eliminate emotional bias. When you tap any game, Edge Scanner calculates offensive and defensive efficiency, pace of play, and net ratings—paired with responsible gaming volatility warnings. It enforces 1-2% unit sizing so you never blow your bankroll on tilt."*

### 1:50 – 2:00 | Conclusion & Class Demo

## 💡 Why This App Is Important

1. **Information Asymmetry:** Sportsbooks have millions in algorithmic infrastructure. The everyday consumer has only intuition. Edge Scanner levels the playing field with transparent market comparison.
2. **Eliminating the 'Vig' Tax:** A bettor paying -115 instead of -108 over a 100-game season loses hundreds in dead money to bookmaker commissions. Line shopping is the foundational requirement of sports analytics.
3. **Environmental Blindspots:** Wind over 15 mph is historically the single most predictive factor for lower NFL totals, yet almost no mobile sportsbook shows weather on the betting screen.
4. **Responsible Play & Consumer Protection:** Built-in GPS jurisdiction detection, problem gambling helplines (1-800-GAMBLER), and strict bankroll unit sizing protect users.

## ❓ Rapid Q&A Defense for Classmates & Teacher

- **Q: Does this app scrape sportsbook websites illegally?**
  - *A: No. It uses authorized API proxy gateways (`/api/odds`) and client caching. It never violates bookmaker terms or scrapes protected websites.*
- **Q: Is this encouraging gambling?**
  - *A: On the contrary, it acts as a consumer protection tool. It shows state legal statuses, embeds problem gaming helplines, provides risk variance warnings, and enforces a strict bankroll ledger.*
- **Q: How does the OCR slip scanner work?**
  - *A: It uses client-side optical character recognition. It runs directly in the browser, extracting wager data locally without uploading personal receipts to any third-party marketing database.*
